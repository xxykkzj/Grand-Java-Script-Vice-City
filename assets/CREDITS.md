## Notes

- **Kenney starter kits** (City Builder, Racing, 3D Platformer): models and `Textures/colormap.png` taken from the official KenneyNL GitHub repositories, MIT licensed. Keep the copyright notice and licence text with redistributions.
- **Kenney City Kit Suburban (1.0)** and **Car Kit (2.0)**: official downloads, CC0 1.0. Car Kit models share `Textures/colormap.png`; Suburban models use plain material colours.
- **Quaternius Street Pack**: official download, CC0 1.0 (consider supporting the author on Patreon). The pack ships FBX/OBJ/Blend only; the OBJ+MTL files were converted to GLB with Cesium obj2gltf (Apache-2.0, not included) via `tools/convert-obj.mjs`. Geometry and material colours are unchanged; the combined overview `Streets_all` was skipped.
- **RobotExpressive**: model by Tomas Laulhe (https://www.patreon.com/quaternius), CC0 1.0. Modifications by Don McCurdy: added three facial-expression morph targets, converted with FBX2GLTF, removed duplicate materials and reduced metalness. Attribution is not required but appreciated; consider supporting the creator.

Any CC-BY asset added later must be listed here with author, URL, licence and a note of any changes.
